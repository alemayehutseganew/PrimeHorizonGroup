Our story

From a shared idea to a registered company

Prime Horizon Group was conceived by a group of 14 professionals who saw the same problem from different sides: Ethiopian businesses struggle to find transport operators who communicate, and technical contractors who document. We incorporated as a private limited company to close both gaps at once.

Today the company operates two divisions — Transport & Logistics and Technical Services — sharing one management system that tracks every vehicle, project, invoice and shareholder contribution. That system is the same discipline we bring to client work.

Milestones

Our roadmap so far

Phase 1

Founding agreement

The founding group agrees the business model, share structure and initial capital plan across transport and technical services.

Phase 2

Incorporation & licensing

Registration as a private limited company, tax and banking setup, and industry licensing for transport and electrical works.

Phase 3

Capital call & fleet acquisition

Shareholders meet their capital commitments; the first vehicles are acquired, insured and commissioned into service.

Phase 4

Operations launch

Transport contracts begin; the technical division takes its first IT, networking and electrical projects.

Phase 5

Scale & new services

Expansion into additional regions and service lines — power solutions, software products and long-term support contracts.
