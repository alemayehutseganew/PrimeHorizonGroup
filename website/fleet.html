A fleet managed like an asset — not like a gamble.

Every vehicle in our fleet has a service history, valid insurance, an assigned driver and a trip record. That is how we keep your load moving and your cost predictable.

Vehicle categories

Capacity for cargo, people and everything between

Fleet composition grows with the company’s capital programme. Current and planned categories are listed below.

Heavy Trucks

18–28 tonne rigid and articulated trucks for full-truckload haulage on long-haul corridors.

  • Dry van and curtain-side bodies
  • Long-haul route assignments
  • Odometer-tracked trip records

Light & Medium Trucks

Pickups and 3–10 tonne box trucks for urban deliveries, regional distribution and site logistics.

  • City and regional distribution
  • Timed delivery windows
  • Proof-of-delivery capture

Passenger Vehicles

Minibuses, coasters and saloon cars for staff shuttles, airport transfers and group travel.

  • Staff transport contracts
  • Airport and event transfers
  • Professional, licensed drivers
